# AcornOps Website

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is infrastructure engineers, platform engineers, SREs, and technical leaders evaluating an operations platform for Kubernetes clusters and Linux VMs. They arrive with healthy skepticism, often while comparing architecture, security boundaries, and incident workflows. They need to understand how AcornOps works, see authentic product evidence, and reach a live demo or documentation without marketing friction.

## Product Purpose

This site explains AcornOps as a self-hosted operations platform that follows an operational signal through investigation, governed action, and inspectable evidence. Its primary conversion is the public demo. Documentation and GitHub provide secondary validation paths. Success means a technically informed visitor can understand the system boundary, trust the product claims, and choose a next step without needing JavaScript.

## Brand Personality

Warm, mechanical, and assured. AcornOps should feel like an operator's field guide: precise enough for incident work, tactile enough to be memorable, and candid enough to earn technical trust. The mascot carries curiosity and momentum without making production operations feel childish.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS feature-card grids, invented metrics, testimonials, pricing tables, dark observability dashboards, green cloud artwork, glass effects, gradient text, terminal cosplay, and claims of fully autonomous remediation. Do not turn the page into an editorial magazine or a collection of floating rounded cards.

## Design Principles

1. Follow one operational story from symptom to verified resolution.
2. Show the actual console and architecture instead of substituting claims.
3. Make governance and inspectability visible at every consequential step.
4. Let the canonical brand system carry personality without weakening technical credibility.
5. Keep the primary path to the demo obvious, fast, and resilient.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve content and navigation with JavaScript disabled. Support keyboard navigation, strong focus visibility, semantic landmarks and headings, 44-pixel touch targets, reduced motion, coarse pointers, zoom and reflow, and screen-reader-safe text animation. Never rely on color or motion alone to communicate incident state.
